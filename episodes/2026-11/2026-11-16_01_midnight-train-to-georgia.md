## The story · Die Geschichte

„Midnight Train to Georgia“ began as a midnight plane to Houston. In 1970 Jim Weatherly called a friend, someone else answered and said she was taking the midnight plane to Houston. He wrote the song in under an hour. A singer whose family came from Georgia wanted it, but her people didn't take planes. So the plane became a train, and in 1973 Gladys Knight & the Pips took it to number one.

In K'Town a timetable just appeared on a lamppost on Main Street. One train. No time.

**Emmy asks:** Who in K'Town takes the last train?
